# Portfolio 1: Database Design and Modelling

**Course:** MIT8103 Advanced Database Systems

**Case Study:** NaijaOpenLearner Online Store

**Project Type:** Simulated E-Commerce Platform

## Business Overview

NaijaOpenLearner Online Store is a fictional online business selling educational products, including textbooks, stationery, calculators and learning kits.

The proposed database will manage customers, products, orders, payments, inventory and deliveries.

## Business Problem

Without a properly structured database, the business could experience duplicated customer information, inaccurate stock records, inconsistent order and payment information, unreliable sales reports and difficulties tracking deliveries.

## Portfolio 1 Objective

Design a relational database for the online store, including an Entity-Relationship (ER) diagram, relational schema, primary and foreign keys, appropriate constraints and an explanation of database normalisation.

**Status:** In Progress

**Data:** All customer, product and transaction records used in this project will be fictional.
